
## Desktop anonymizer (Nov 2026) — same identity, offline app window
Locked: Redaction Field tokens unchanged; the **vault (night) theme is the default**, vellum is the toggle
(the owner works dark). Fonts are bundled locally (`app/desktop/ui/fonts`, Geist Sans/Mono variable,
Cyrillic covered) — the app loads nothing from the network.
- **Custody split** is the layout primitive, repeated on every working screen: left = *on this computer*
  (real values on a carbon rule) → dashed perimeter «далі — лише мітки» → right = *what goes to the AI*
  (labels as seal chips «Фізособа-1»). Anonymize preview, restore preview and the unlock note all use it.
- Label chip = the atom, now human-readable (`Фізособа-1`), mono, seal-cyan number.
- Project rail on the left (cases), ledger tables for labels/journal, stamps for categories (toggle stamps).
- Motion: the carbon bar sweeps over a value and leaves the label (clip-path 520 ms) on preview reveal;
  chips seal with a 70 ms stagger; restored values get a seal underline that draws in. Reduced motion = end state.
